What is Scientology?

An informational guide to Scientology, the religious movement L. Ron Hubbard founded in the 1950s: its history, beliefs, texts, symbols, and practices, and the debates that surround it today.

Where the Church's own account and outside research disagree, this site presents both, with sources for every claim.

Start reading See the sources

For Hubbard, the process of freeing the individual is the fundamental purpose of religion.

“For countless ages, a goal of religion has been the salvage of the human spirit. Man has tried by many practices to find the pathway to salvation. He has held the imperishable hope that someday in some way he would be free.”
L. Ron Hubbard, quoted in Melton

Nine steps, like the Bridge

Hubbard mapped the path to spiritual freedom as the Bridge to Total Freedom, a series of steps each Scientologist climbs. (Urban) This site is laid out the same way, in nine sections from the basics to the debates.
