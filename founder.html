Founder

L. Ron Hubbard, writer, explorer of the mind, and founder

Before he founded a religion, L. Ron Hubbard was a popular science fiction writer with a restless curiosity about the human mind.

In 1950 he published Dianetics, which laid out his ideas about why people suffer and how they can be freed from it. Within a few years he took those ideas further, into the spiritual realm.

From Dianetics to Scientology
