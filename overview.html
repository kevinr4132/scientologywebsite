Overview

A religion built for the questions of modern life

Who am I? Where did I come from? What happens next? Scientology offers practical answers to life's oldest questions.

Founded in the United States by L. Ron Hubbard, Scientology grew out of Dianetics, his 1950 book about the mind. By the end of 1953, Hubbard had expanded that work into a full religion.

The big idea

Scientology teaches that each person is an immortal spiritual being, called a thetan. Through study and counseling, members move up a path called the Bridge to Total Freedom toward greater awareness and ability.

Why people are drawn to it

Scientology presents itself as an applied religion: not just something to believe, but something to use. Members describe it as a set of tools for improving their relationships, work, and sense of purpose.
