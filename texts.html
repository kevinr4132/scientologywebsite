Texts

Answers written down, so anyone can study them

In Scientology, the writings of L. Ron Hubbard are treated as scripture.

Members don't rely on interpretation handed down by a priest. They study Hubbard's own words directly, working through his books and lectures as they move up the Bridge.

Dianetics (1950)
Hubbard's first major book and the starting point for millions of readers, introducing his ideas about engrams and the mind.
