Texts

The writings of L. Ron Hubbard

In Scientology, all of L. Ron Hubbard's writings on the subject, both published and unpublished, are considered scripture. (Melton)

Key texts

The Original Thesis (1948)
Hubbard's first written statement of his conclusions about the mind. (Melton)
"Dianetics: The Evolution of a Science" (1950)
An article in Astounding Science Fiction that drew such a positive response that Hubbard expanded it into a book. (Lewis)
Dianetics: The Modern Science of Mental Health (1950)
The bestseller that started the movement, presenting techniques to free the mind of engrams. (Lewis)
The Creation of Human Ability and The Phoenix Lectures
Works in which Hubbard guides auditors in helping the thetan experience itself outside the body. (Urban)
Fundamentals of Thought
A work in which Hubbard presents Scientology as a science that can be demonstrated. (Lewis)
The OT levels
The most sacred teachings of Scientology, kept confidential and revealed only to members who train at those levels. (Melton)

Publishing and translation

The Church translated Hubbard's writings into many languages as it spread across Europe in the 1970s. (Melton) Today, its own publishing outlets, including Bridge Publications, Inc. and Freedom magazine, publish his work. (Urban)
