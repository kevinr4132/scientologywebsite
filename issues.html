Issues

The hard questions, and how the Church answers them

No religion draws this much attention without controversy. Here are the questions people ask most, alongside the Church's response.

Why are some teachings confidential?

Some higher-level teachings are only available to members who have advanced far enough on the Bridge. Critics call this secrecy. The Church says these materials are sacred and are meant to be studied in order, once a member is ready for them.

Is it really a religion?

The Church fought long legal battles with the U.S. government over whether it qualifies as a tax-exempt religion. The Church points to its eventual recognition as proof of its religious status.

What about the leaked materials?

Confidential Scientology materials have been leaked and posted online. The Church has treated these as stolen scripture and gone to court to stop their publication.

How many members are there, really?

The Church reports millions; independent surveys report far fewer. See Demographics for both numbers.
