Demographics

From one book in 1950 to churches around the world

10,000,000
Members the Church reports worldwide
vs.
25,000
Independent survey estimate, U.S. adults only, 2008

The Church reports millions of members worldwide, all grown from a single book published in 1950.

Independent surveys count far fewer Scientologists. Those surveys measure only U.S. adults who name Scientology as their religion, so they don't capture the Church's worldwide reach or people who practice alongside another faith. The true number remains debated.

The numbers side by side

Church figure (worldwide)about 10,000,000
Survey estimate (U.S. adults, 2001)about 55,000
Survey estimate (U.S. adults, 2008)about 25,000

Bars are drawn to scale. The Church's figure counts members worldwide; the survey figures count U.S. adults only.

Check these numbers against your sources and cite them. The U.S. estimates come from the American Religious Identification Survey (ARIS); if you use them, add it to your bibliography.
