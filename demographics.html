In about seventy years, Scientology grew from a single bestselling book into a worldwide church.

How it spread

  • 1950s: Dianetics becomes a bestseller, and groups form across America to practice Hubbard's techniques. There was a Dianetics "fad" for several years, attracting even intellectuals like Aldous Huxley. (Lewis)
  • 1970s: The Church spreads across Europe and begins translating Hubbard's writings into many languages. (Melton)
  • 1980s: Growth continues, and after the fall of the Berlin Wall the Church quickly spreads into eastern Europe. (Melton)
  • Today: The Church operates in more than 150 countries. (Melton)

Well-known members

A number of celebrities have embraced Scientology's teachings, including Tom Cruise, John Travolta, and Kirstie Alley. (Melton) The Church's connection to the entertainment industry is one of the things the public knows best about it. (Lewis)

How many Scientologists are there?

The Church reports millions of members worldwide, while independent surveys count far fewer. Those surveys measure only U.S. adults who name Scientology as their religion, so they don't capture the Church's worldwide reach.
