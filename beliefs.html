Beliefs

You are not your past. You are a spiritual being.

At the heart of Scientology is a hopeful idea: what holds you back can be found, faced, and removed.

Engrams
Painful memories that Scientology teaches are stored in the mind and quietly cause problems in a person's life.
Auditing
One-on-one spiritual counseling that helps a person locate and remove their engrams.
Clear
The state a person reaches once they are free of engrams, which Scientologists see as a major milestone.
Thetan
The immortal spiritual self. Scientology teaches that this, not the body or mind, is who you truly are.

The Bridge to Total Freedom

Together, these ideas form a clear path that members climb one step at a time.
