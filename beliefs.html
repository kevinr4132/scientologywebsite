Beliefs

You are not your past. You are a spiritual being.

At the heart of Scientology is a hopeful idea: whatever holds you back can be found, faced, and removed.

Life is trying to survive

Hubbard believed the basic principle of human existence is survival. Actions that support survival are good and bring pleasure, while actions against survival are destructive. (Melton)

“I suddenly realized that survival was the pin on which you could hang the rest of this ... that life, all life, is trying to survive.”

L. Ron Hubbard, quoted in Melton

Key ideas

The analytical mind
The part of the mind that, under normal conditions, makes clear, survival-oriented judgments. (Melton)
The reactive mind
The part that takes over when the mind isn't fully functioning. It stores painful experiences and can cause a person to act irrationally. (Melton)
Engrams
Stored images of painful experiences. When something later reminds a person of one, it can trigger negative emotions and harmful actions. (Melton)
Auditing
One-on-one counseling in which an auditor helps a person bring engrams to the surface, confront them, and eliminate them. (Melton)
The E-meter
An instrument that measures a small electrical current passing through the body. According to Church teaching, its readings help locate stored engrams. (Melton)
Clear
The state reached once a person is free of engrams, a major goal for every Scientologist. (Melton)
The thetan
The true, spiritual self, which can exist apart from the body and has lived in other bodies before this one, similar to reincarnation in Eastern religions. (Melton)

Where the thetan came from

Hubbard taught that thetans began billions of years ago with an original Cause. Their interaction created MEST (matter, energy, space, and time), the physical universe. Over time, thetans became trapped in MEST and forgot their creative abilities and who they truly were, until their journey brought them to Earth. (Melton) Through Scientology, members come to understand themselves as spiritual beings once again.

The Bridge to Total Freedom

These ideas form a clear path that members climb one step at a time.

EngramsPainful memories hold a person back
AuditingCounseling finds and removes them
ClearFree of engrams
Operating ThetanFully aware and functioning as a spiritual being

Scientology and God

Scientology does not tell members what to believe about God. As members grow aware of the higher dynamics of life, they are free to reach their own conclusions about God's nature. (Melton) That freedom does not mean belief in God is unimportant:

“No culture in the history of the world, save the thoroughly depraved ones, has failed to affirm the existence of a Supreme Being.”

L. Ron Hubbard, quoted in Melton
