Life Stages

A life measured in steps on the Bridge, and beyond this lifetime

For Scientologists, the most important stages of life are the steps of the Bridge to Total Freedom, Hubbard's map of the complete path to spiritual freedom. (Urban)

Climbing the Bridge

The Bridge lists fifteen Operating Thetan (OT) levels above Clear, though only eight appear to have been completed by Hubbard before his death. (Urban) Each level is delivered at a different kind of church: (Melton)

Auditing and trainingAt local churches and missions, members learn the basics and receive auditing.
ClearFree of engrams. Members who reach Clear may go on to the OT levels.
OT I–VTaught at Advanced Organization centers.
OT VI–VIITaught at the Flag Service Organization in Clearwater, Florida.
OT VIIITaught aboard the ship Freewinds.

Read from bottom to top, the way members climb. All levels as described in Melton.

Beyond one lifetime

Scientologists believe the thetan has lived many lifetimes in a human body and will live more in the future. A member who doesn't reach the top of the Bridge in this life can continue climbing in the next. (Lewis)

Rites of passage and gatherings

Like other religions, Scientology marks life's milestones with ceremonies, including weddings, namings, and funerals. Members also gather for large celebrations called "Events," which honor the Church and its founder. (Lewis) Churches also hold a weekly Sunday service.

Pilgrimage

Some Scientologists travel to places connected with Hubbard's life and work, a practice scholar Donald Westbrook describes as "walking in Ron's footsteps." (Lewis)
