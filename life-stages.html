Life Stages

Life as a journey upward, one step at a time

For Scientologists, life's major stages are measured by progress on the Bridge to Total Freedom.

Moving up the Bridge

Members progress through auditing and training, aiming first for the state of Clear and then for higher levels of spiritual awareness as a thetan. Each step builds on the last.

The Sunday service

Every week, Scientology churches hold a Sunday service, bringing members together alongside their individual auditing and study.
